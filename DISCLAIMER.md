# Disclaimer

Read all of this before you flash.

**Not Elektron's.** This is an independent, unofficial project. It is not made, endorsed, supported or reviewed by Elektron Music Machines. Elektron, Syntakt, Transfer and Overbridge are Elektron's trademarks, used here only to say what this works with. Do not ask Elektron's support about problems with a modded unit.

**At your own risk.** The software is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). Flashing modified firmware can leave a unit misbehaving or not starting, and may void your warranty. The mod never changes the bootloader or the updater, and the official OS can be flashed back from the boot menu, but nobody can promise that holds for every unit or every failure.

**Your data.** The mod reads and writes the +Drive: sample banks, takes, and space past the banks for unsaved takes. Back up your projects and samples with Transfer before flashing, and again before every update of the mod. Formats may change between releases with no way back.

**Tested on very little hardware.** Every feature runs in emulation first, but emulation does not model timing, caches or the unit's FPGAs. Everything has run on one unit, often only one person's patterns. Your unit may differ.

**No firmware here.** This repository and its releases contain none of Elektron's firmware: only our code and the places it goes. You get the official OS from Elektron yourself, under Elektron's terms, and the patcher builds the modded file on your machine.

**Do not share modded OS files.** A patched `.syx` contains Elektron's firmware. Share the patch, not the result. Pull requests, issues and discussions that attach or paste firmware, or bytes taken from it, are deleted.

**Live use.** Do not rely on a modded unit for a show without testing your exact set on it first.
